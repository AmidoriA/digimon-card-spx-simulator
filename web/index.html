          How to play

          Each player brings a 30-card deck. Hands are open: both players can see each other's cards. You win by scoring three KOs, or when the opponent can no longer field a Digimon.

          A turn belongs to one player

          1. Preparation. The turn player draws up to 4 cards (only they draw). With no Digimon in play they must send one out from their hand. Any level may enter, but a level C entering directly has HP and attack powers halved, and a level U has them quartered. They may change all cards (discard the hand and redraw) while the Online deck has cards. A hand with no Digimon card is redrawn automatically; if the deck is empty, that player loses.
          2. Digivolve. In this order: send one Digimon card to the DP Slot (adds its +P), use a digivolve option card, then digivolve normally: R to C to U, same Specialty, DP at least the new card's cost. Digivolving gives the new card's full HP and empties the DP Slot. Using an option card ends the phase.
          3. Battle. If the opponent has no Digimon there is no battle. Otherwise both players secretly choose an attack (○ △ ✕). Then the opponent commits a support card, and after seeing it the turn player commits theirs. A support can be a Digimon or option card from the hand, or the unseen top card of the Online deck. Supports and ✕ effects resolve, then the turn player strikes first.

          Support timing

          Every effect has a speed tier. Tier 3 resolves first, then 2, then 1. Inside a tier: option cards (turn player first), then Digimon supports, then ✕ effects. "Support voided" stops Digimon supports only; "Support and Option Effects voided" stops both.

          ✕ attack effects

          • 1st Attack strikes first regardless of turn.
          • ○/△/✕ to 0 lowers that attack of the opponent to 0.
          • Counter if the opponent used the named attack, it misses and you strike second with its power.
          • Crash attack power becomes your HP, then your HP becomes 10.
          • Eat-up HP recover HP equal to the damage inflicted.
          • Jamming voids the opponent's Digimon support effect.
          • Foe x3 triples your ✕ power against that Specialty.

          KOs

          A KO'd Digimon and its digivolution stack go Offline and the opponent scores. An armor Digimon falls back to its Rookie form instead. A revive effect keeps the Digimon in play, except against the KO that would decide the match.
